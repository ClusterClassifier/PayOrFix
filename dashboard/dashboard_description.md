# PayOrFix Dashboard Guide

## Purpose

The dashboard identifies whether employee attrition is more closely associated with compensation-related factors or structural workplace factors, and shows how the pattern differs by department.

## KPI Cards

- **Attrition Rate:** Percentage of employees who left the organisation.
- **Total Employees:** Number of employees included in the analysis.
- **Highest Attrition Department:** Department with the highest attrition rate.
- **Overtime Share of Attrition:** Share of leavers who worked overtime.

## Charts

- **Compensational and Structural Attrition Gap:** Compares leavers and stayers across compensation variables and structural variables for each department. A positive gap means leavers have a higher average value; a negative gap means leavers have a lower average value.
- **Attrition Gap by Factor:** Shows the individual factors with the largest differences between leavers and stayers. Cyan represents compensation factors; amber represents structural factors.
- **Attrition Rate by Department:** Compares the percentage of employees who left in each department.

## Department Slicer

Use the slicer on the left to filter the dashboard by department. Select one department to inspect its attrition rate and factor gaps, or select multiple departments for comparison. Clear the selection to return to the full-company view.
